In pictures: Me & Em expands into jewellery

Me & Em has today (4 September) unveiled its debut jewellery collection, marking a "new era of luxury" for the premium womenswear brand.

By Chloe Mills 4 September 2026

Me & Em‘s new 15-piece “Atelier” collection has been designed in London and made in Italy, crafted in 24kt gold plate.

The collection includes multi-wear pendant earrings, adjustable necklaces, stacking rings and statement cuffs.

Prices start at £150 for a signet ring and range up to £695 for a link necklace.

Me & Em said the collection is “true to the brand’s codes”, and has been designed as a “forever collection” to complement its core clothing offer.

The jewellery launch comes amid Me & Em’s ongoing growth of its accessories category, which also includes bags, hats, belts, scarves, socks, tights and sunglasses.

Advertisement

Me & Em is also opening a pop-up store in Geneva, Switzerland, later this month – the pop-up will mark its first European physical retail presence outside of its UK store estate.

Me & Em posted a 24% rise in revenues year on year to £147.9m for the year to January 2025.
