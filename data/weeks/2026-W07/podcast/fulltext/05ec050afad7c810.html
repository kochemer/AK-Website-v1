    1.10-carat round brilliant-cut C7 SI1 Australian Chocolate Diamond Ring

    Posted February 11, 2026 |
    Crafted in 18-carat yellow gold, this striking ring showcases a 1.10-carat round brilliant-cut C7 SI1 Australian Chocolate Diamond, elegantly secured in a half-bezel setting.

    From Bolton Gems, the band is accented with grain-set round brilliant-cut white diamonds, adding refined sparkle and totalling 0.32-carats.

    More information: Bolton Gems
