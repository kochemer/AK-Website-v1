    2.42-carat round Inverell Sapphire & Diamond Ring

    Posted February 11, 2026 |
    Crafted in 18-carat yellow and white gold, this elegant trilogy ring showcases a breathtaking 2.42-carat round Inverell Sapphire, claw set in white gold.

    A round brilliant cut diamond sits on each side, also claw set, adding balance and brilliance, with a total diamond weight of 0.62-carats. The design is finished with a polished yellow gold band.

    More information: Bolton Gems
