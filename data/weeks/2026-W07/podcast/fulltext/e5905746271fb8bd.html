    2.21-carat pear-cut C5 SI2 Australian Chocolate Diamond Ring

    Posted February 11, 2026 |
    Crafted in 18-carat yellow gold, this elegant ring showcases a striking 2.21-carat pear-cut C5 SI2 Australian Chocolate Diamond.

    The elongated pear shape is beautifully cradled in a flowing setting that highlights the stone’s warm champagne tones and natural brilliance. Bold yet refined, this design is a captivating statement piece that celebrates the unique beauty of Australian Chocolate Diamonds.
