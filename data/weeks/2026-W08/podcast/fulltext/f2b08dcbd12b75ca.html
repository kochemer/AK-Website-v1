What is Sarvam AI’s Indus: India’s answer to ChatGPT, Gemini-like chatbots?

Synopsis

Indian AI startup Sarvam.AI has launched Indus, a new chat interface. This chatbot is built on Sarvam's own 105 billion sovereign model. Indus is positioned as a competitor to global AI giants like OpenAI's ChatGPT and Google's Gemini. The company is currently focused on accuracy and usefulness for the Indian context.
India’s artificial intelligence (AI) startup Sarvam.AI announced Indus, a chat interface on Saturday. The AI chatbot is built on Sarvam’s 105 billion sovereign model, and is being pitched as a competitor against OpenAI’s ChatGPT and Google's Gemini.

“At this stage, we are intentionally focused on accuracy, usefulness, efficiency, and alignment for the Indian context before training bigger foundational models,” the company wrote in a blogpost.

For now the interface is available on both iOS and Android.


Indus has been launched in limited beta, due to limited compute capacity, as the startup awaits to receive and eventually address feedback from users.

The startup has flagged that this will lead to some users initially encountering a waitlist.

The interface resembles that of ChatGPT with similar fonts and colour size.

“Also, we believe Sovereign AI must be built with the country, not just for it. That means learning from the people who understand India best - its everyday users, developers, researchers, and creators. So, try out the app and let us know what works well and what doesn't. We are in listen mode,” Pratyush Kumar, Sarvam AI co-founder wrote in a post on X.

At the time of writing, the company has seen over 10,000 downloads of the chatbot app on Google Playstore. The app is designated for users aged 13 and above, marking a clear contrast with several American competitors that have reportedly faced allegations of negatively affecting minors’ mental health.
