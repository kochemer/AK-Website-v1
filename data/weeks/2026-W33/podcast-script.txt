Speaking of digital transformation, identity management is taking center stage in customer experience, especially in luxury and high-touch retail.

Forrester has just released its Q3 2026 Customer Identity and Access Management, or CIAM, landscape report, and it’s packed with insights that anyone in luxury, ecommerce, or consumer retail should pay attention to. The report highlights three major drivers shaping the CIAM sector: the growing demand for seamless and secure customer experiences, the need for regulatory compliance in an increasingly complex digital environment, and, perhaps most intriguingly, the emergence of AI agents as a new identity type. This development is particularly relevant for luxury brands, where trust, privacy, and personalization are paramount. As consumers interact with brands across multiple channels—online, mobile, in-store—the challenge is to create a unified, secure identity that enables personalized service while safeguarding privacy. The introduction of AI agents as identity types signals a future where digital assistants or bots might interact on behalf of customers, making purchases, booking appointments, or even negotiating offers. This raises important questions about authentication, consent, and the customer journey. Forrester’s report suggests that brands that invest in robust, flexible CIAM solutions will be better positioned to deliver the high-touch, secure experiences that luxury customers expect. In the broader context, this is part of a larger shift toward AI-driven personalization and automation. As the lines between human and digital agents blur, the ability to manage identity and access securely becomes a strategic differentiator. For luxury and consumer brands, the message is clear: invest in smart, adaptive CIAM now, or risk falling behind as the customer landscape evolves at lightning speed.
