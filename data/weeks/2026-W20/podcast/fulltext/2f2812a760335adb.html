Beaumont Jewelry Collective Partners with Gem Legacy to Support East African Mining Communities

A portion of profits supports Gem Legacy’s education and community programs in East Africa’s gemstone mining regions.

Published

1 day ago

on

May 15, 2026

By

Press Releases
  • Share
  • Tweet

(PRESS RELEASE) FERNDALE, MI — Beaumont Jewelry Collective (BJC), an e-commerce platform founded by Laura and Chloe Beaumont and launched in 2025, has selected Gem Legacy as one of its nonprofit beneficiaries. A percentage of the profits from the Collective will help fund Gem Legacy’s education, training, and community development programs in East Africa’s gemstone mining regions.

Beaumont Jewelry Collective features ten independent designers: Delphine Leymarie, Dylann Wolfe, Kathe Mai Designs, Karin Jacobson, KIma, M. Hisae, Mazahri, Mercurius Jewelry, Twyla Dill, and WEND Jewelry. BJC chose Gem Legacy for its direct, measurable work strengthening the future of artisanal mining communities.

“Partnerships like this reflect a growing commitment among jewelry designers to keep the people and communities behind the gemstones visible, valued, and supported,” said Bernadette Mack, Executive Director of Gem Legacy. “When designers invest in the communities behind the gems their clients love, they help create a stronger, more responsible jewelry industry from miner to maker to consumer.”

Gem Legacy’s programs focus on education, health, and economic empowerment in Tanzania and Kenya. Current initiatives include:

  • The Gem Legacy Campus, under construction in Arusha, Tanzania, expanding access to vocational education for youth in mining communities.
  • The Miner Toolkit Program, which equips small-scale artisanal miners with proper tools, gear, and health & safety education.
  • Scholarship and meal programs, which keep hundreds of students in school and on a path toward long-term stability and success.

“We believe every piece of jewelry tells a story,” said Laura Beaumont, co-founder of Beaumont Jewelry Collective. “Ours begins with transparency and ends with impact. Supporting Gem Legacy lets our customers connect their purchases directly to opportunities for others.”

This collaboration links artisans, retailers, and consumers to the communities where the jewelry journey begins—turning design into development and beauty into progress.

Beaumont Jewelry Collective joins more than a dozen other jewelry-industry companies that have partnered to donate a specific portion of their profits to benefit Gem Legacy’s mission and programs. To find out more about partnering with Gem Legacy, contact Bernadette Mack at [email protected].
