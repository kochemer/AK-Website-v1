    Posted May 07, 2026 |
    A celebration of Australia’s natural treasures, brought together in jewellery rich with heritage, rarity, and craftsmanship.

    Soklich & Co celebrates distinctly Australian fine jewellery, pairing pearls, native gold nuggets, opals and Argyle-sourced diamonds to create pendants that carry both provenance and craftsmanship in equal measure.
