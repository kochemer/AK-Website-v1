Speaking of supply and demand in luxury, there’s a big development in the diamond industry we need to discuss.

[Segment 4: De Beers Slashes One-Third of Sightholders]

Host:  
The De Beers Group, a titan in the diamond sector, has significantly reduced its number of sightholders—the select dealers authorized to purchase rough diamonds—by about one-third, according to Jeweller’s business news.

This move tightens access to De Beers’ diamond supply and could reshape the landscape for jewellery manufacturers and retailers. Fewer sightholders means increased competition among buyers and potentially tighter supply chains.

For luxury jewellery brands and retailers, this could lead to challenges in sourcing, pricing fluctuations, and a need for closer relationships with suppliers. It may also prompt some companies to diversify sourcing strategies or invest more in lab-grown diamonds.

From a market perspective, the consolidation reflects De Beers’ intent to streamline distribution and perhaps reinforce the exclusivity and value proposition of its diamonds.

We’ll be watching how this impacts pricing, availability, and ultimately consumer demand in the months ahead.
